## Privacy Policy (Template)

This template is provided to help you publish the MVP to Google Play. You must review and adapt it to your actual data practices and legal requirements.

### Summary
This app helps you create scheduled tasks and reminders. We collect the minimum data needed to provide accounts, sync tasks, and track streaks.

### Data we collect
- **Account data**: email address.
- **App data you create**: task titles, schedules (time, repeat pattern), timezone, completion events, streak counters.
- **Device data (optional)**: push notification token (if/when push notifications are enabled).

### Why we collect it
- **Provide service**: create an account, sign in, sync tasks across devices.
- **Reminders**: deliver reminders you’ve configured.
- **Streaks**: compute and show streak progress.

### Data sharing
- We do not sell your personal data.\n+- We do not share your personal data with third parties for their marketing.\n+- Service providers may process data to run the infrastructure (hosting and database).

### Data retention
- We retain your data while your account is active.\n+- You can delete your account in-app. Account deletion deletes associated data from our servers within a reasonable timeframe.

### Security
- Passwords are stored as strong salted hashes.\n+- Data is transmitted over HTTPS in production.

### Your controls
- **Account deletion**: available inside the app.\n+- **Notification controls**: you can disable notifications in Android settings, and you can disable exact alarms inside the app settings.
